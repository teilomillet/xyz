Effective date: June 5, 2026

Bumpies is a strategy board game for iPhone and iPad by Teilo Millet.

## Data collection

Bumpies does not collect personal data for advertising, tracking, analytics, or resale.

The app stores gameplay preferences and progress locally on your device. This includes settings such as sound, haptics, selected rule mode, AI difficulty, tutorial state, and local progress records. This information is stored with Apple-provided on-device storage and is not sent to a Bumpies server.

## Game Center

Bumpies uses Apple Game Center for online multiplayer, achievements, and leaderboards. When you use Game Center features, Apple may process your Game Center account, player identifier, display name, match data, achievements, leaderboard scores, and related Game Center information under Apple's privacy terms.

Bumpies uses Game Center only to provide those gameplay features. Bumpies does not operate its own account system and does not store Game Center information on a separate developer server.

## Tracking and advertising

Bumpies does not show ads and does not use third-party tracking SDKs.

Bumpies does not request permission to track you across apps and websites owned by other companies.

## Children

Bumpies is not directed to children as a separate child-specific service. The app does not knowingly collect personal information from children.
